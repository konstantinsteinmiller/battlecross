## How to find what is missing again

1. `pnpm art:status` lists every id with no painted file.
2. `pnpm art:prompts` rewrites `art-sheets/PAINT-STATUS.md`: every sheet that is not ✓ is a row above.
3. A new item or Infernal twin enters the catalogue by `ART_CATALOGUE` in `src/game/art/artSheet.ts`; export its reference sheet with
   `pnpm art:export --only <stem>`, paint it with the Art Desk (`pnpm art:desk`), slice and compress come with it.
