# Credits

## Music

**"The Last Light"** (the cinematic trailer's score and the boss-fight music,
`public/audio/music/boss.ogg`) is composed in `tools/score-render/score.mjs`
and rendered with instrument samples from:

- **FluidR3_GM** General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, licensed under
  [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/),
  as converted and published by
  [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (Benjamin Gleitzman).
  Instruments used: acoustic grand piano, violin, cello, string ensemble,
  pizzicato strings, nylon acoustic guitar, taiko drum, timpani.

All other music and every sound effect are synthesized by the game's own code.
