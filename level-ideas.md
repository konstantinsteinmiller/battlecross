# Level ideas

Proposals to make a zone worth entering and exploring. Nothing here is built
unless it is ticked in `roadmap.md` (#54 to #59 are the first batch). Each idea
names what it costs to build on the existing zone generator.

## Things to find

- **A treasure map piece** in a chest; three pieces mark a dig spot in another
  zone (a reason to revisit a cleared zone).
- **A locked chest and its key** carried by a named enemy somewhere in the
  zone (the key-bearer has a small key icon over the head).
- **A wandering merchant** who appears in one zone per visit with one rare
  item, shown on the world map with a small cart.
- **Lore stones**: one per zone, a two-line bubble of story; reading all
  twelve unlocks a cosmetic or a title.
- **Shrines**: step in for a blessing that lasts the visit (more damage,
  faster mana, a shield) or a risky one (double gold, half health).
- **Buried loot** under a suspicious mound, a cracked wall or a lone cross of
  stones; a short hold opens it.
- **Fishing spot** at a pond: one cast per visit, a fish that heals or a boot.
- **Rare animal** (a golden hare, a white stag) that flees when seen; catching
  it drops a gem.

## Things to do besides fighting

- **Pressure plates in order** (built first): three or four plates with a
  symbol each, the order carved on a nearby stone.
- **Light the braziers**: carry fire from one to the next before it burns out.
- **Push blocks** onto marked tiles to open a gate (two or three blocks).
- **Lever and bridge**: a lever on one bank lowers a bridge on another path.
- **Follow the fireflies** to a hidden clearing at night-themed zones.
- **Rescue a captive**: a caged villager; the guard has the key; the villager
  runs to town and gives a discount or an item later.
- **Escort a pack mule** across the zone for a merchant; it stops when enemies
  are near.
- **Timed chest**: opens a short race across the zone to a second chest.
- **Bell or gong** that calls every enemy nearby: a risk taken on purpose for
  a better reward.

## Terrain

- **Elevation, water, tunnels and caves** (built first).
- **Bridges and fords**: narrow crossings that make a fight a choke point.
- **Cliffs with a view**: a ledge from which the boss arena is visible below.
- **Breakable scenery**: barrels, crates and pots that drop a little gold.
- **Hazard ground by theme**: lava vents in the crags, thin ice on the tundra,
  thorn brush in the woods, quicksand in the wastes.
- **Weather and time of day** per visit: rain, fog, dusk; fog shortens how far
  enemies see.
- **One-way drops**: a ledge to jump down that is a shortcut back.
- **Secret walls**: a section of cliff slightly off-colour that an attack
  breaks.
- **Moving platforms or rafts** on a river in one or two zones.

## Enemies and encounters

- **An over-levelled optional enemy** in a side corner (built first).
- **Ambushes**: enemies that rise from brush, snow or the ground when the
  hero crosses a line.
- **Patrols** that walk a route; avoiding them is possible.
- **Sleeping camps**: a pack asleep around a fire; the first strike is a
  guaranteed critical hit.
- **Elite with an aura** that makes its pack stronger: the fight is about
  reaching the elite.
- **Rival adventurer**: a hero-like enemy using the player's own skill set.
- **Two factions fighting each other** when the hero arrives; whoever is left
  is weaker.
- **A mini-boss gate**: a named enemy guards the short path to the boss; the
  long path goes around.
- **Champion bounty**: a wanted poster in town names an enemy in a zone; gold
  on return.
- **Waves at a totem**: destroy the totem to stop reinforcements.

## Reasons to come back to a cleared zone

- **Hard mode** with new enemy mixes and a second loot table.
- **Daily modifier** on one zone (double chests, a rare spawn).
- **Chests that refill** on a long timer, shown on the world map.
- **A changed zone after a decision**: the choice made at a quest changes who
  lives there (already true for the factions; extend it to the scenery).
- **Collection log** per zone: chests found, secrets found, optional enemies
  beaten, shown as three small marks on the world map node.
